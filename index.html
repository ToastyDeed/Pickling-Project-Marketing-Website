Welcome to Raider Relish Co.

Welcome to Raider Relish Co., where school spirit meets artisan flavor! We are a student-run business based out of Richmond Hill High School, crafting premium pickled products with pride. Every jar we produce reflects our commitment to quality, sustainability, and community.

Our Mission

Born right in the heart of Richmond Hill High School, Raider Relish Co. was founded on a simple mission: to bring the school authentic, clean snacks. Driven by teamwork and sustainable practices, we hand pack 100% organic ingredients sourced straight from our school's Green House. We bridge the gap between premium artisan quality and school pride, proving that student-led initiatives can create big, bold flavors that are truly a "big dill."

Why Choose Us?

Every jar of Raider Relish Co. is made by students, for students — and for anyone who loves great food made with care. We use organic ingredients from our own school greenhouse, hand-pack everything in rustic mason jars, and design each label with custom artwork. When you buy from us, you're supporting student entrepreneurship and sustainable agriculture at RHHS.
