Our Process

"We're Kind of a Big Dill"

From Greenhouse to Jar

Every jar of Raider Relish Co. tells a story — one that starts in the RHHS Green House and ends with a delicious, clean snack you can feel good about. Here's how we do it:

1

Grown at RHHS Garden & Greenhouse

All of our cucumbers, beets, onions, garlic, and herbs are grown organically right here on campus at the Richmond Hill High School garden and greenhouse. Students tend the crops from seed to harvest, learning sustainable agriculture hands-on.

2

Hand Pickled

Once harvested, our produce is carefully washed and hand-packed into jars. We use a traditional pickling brine made with organic vinegar, fresh dill, garlic, and our signature spice blend. Every jar is sealed by hand to lock in freshness.

3

Rustic Mason Jars with Custom Labels

Each jar gets a rustic mason jar treatment and a custom-designed label created by RHHS art students. No two labels are exactly alike, making every purchase a one-of-a-kind piece of school pride.

Our Commitment

We believe in transparency, sustainability, and flavor. Every step of our process is student-led, from growing and harvesting to pickling, labeling, and selling. When you choose Raider Relish Co., you're supporting local agriculture, student entrepreneurship, and a greener future.
